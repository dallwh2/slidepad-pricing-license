If you open the same four or five web tools all day on one Mac, buy the lifetime license. Slack, a to-do list, a ticketing system, a dashboard, a notes app: that is the workload SlidePad was built for, and $25 for three machines is not a hard decision.

If you own two or three Macs, the seats are the reason to buy direct. A $8.99 single-device code saves you $16 today and leaves you buying a second license later.

Skip it in these cases:

- **Your Mac is older than macOS 15.** Check the Apple menu, then About This Mac, before anything else. The developer lists macOS 15+ as the requirement, and the older third-party listings that still say 10.14+ are not something to rely on.
- **You are on Windows or iPad.** This is Mac software, full stop.
- **You want a browser replacement.** SlidePad holds the sites you check constantly. It is not built to manage 40 research tabs, and if that is your problem you need a tab manager, not a slide-over panel.
- **You need it for a team.** Three seats per license is the cap I could confirm, and I found no volume or team licensing terms.

**The license arrives by email.** Checkout runs through Stripe and the key goes to the address you typed. A typo there is your problem, so slow down on that field.

**Subscription maths favour the developer, not you.** $2 a month looks trivial and quietly becomes $24 a year, forever. There is no discount for paying annually because there is no annual plan.

**Version information is inconsistent across listings.** You will see 1.0.43 in one place and 1.3.9 in another. Check the version and system requirements on the page you are actually buying from.
